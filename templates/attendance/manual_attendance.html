How this works

The entry is stored as a punch, exactly like one from a terminal, and the day is recalculated straight away — so working hours, lateness and overtime all update.

It is kept separately from device data and labelled in the attendance log with your name and the reason, permanently. Nothing here overwrites what a terminal reported.

A day needs both a check in and a check out before hours can be calculated. If someone forgot to scan out, record the check out.
