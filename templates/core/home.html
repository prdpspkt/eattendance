Everything the front desk and HR both need

One system for the hardware, the rules and the paperwork — so nobody reconciles a spreadsheet against a terminal export at month end.

Real-time push sync

Terminals open outbound connections to your server and deliver punches as they happen. Remote branches work with no VPN, no port forwarding and no static IP.

Many devices, one roster

Register terminals by serial number, watch them come online, and pull a device’s user list or replay its full attendance log on demand.

Shifts and overtime rules

Grace periods, early exits, break deductions, half-days and overtime rounding — applied consistently, so the numbers do not depend on who compiled them.

Leave with balances

Employees request from their own portal, admins approve in one queue, and balances update on approval instead of in a parallel spreadsheet.

Travel orders

Itineraries and expense claims follow the same approval path, and days spent travelling stop being counted as absences.

Reports that reconcile

Monthly attendance, overtime and contact sheets export to Excel and PDF, drawn from the same records the dashboard shows.

Three steps to a live terminal

No agent to install at the branch, and no software on the terminal beyond the firmware it shipped with.

Point the terminal at your server

On the device: Comm. → Ethernet → Cloud Server / ADMS. Enter your hostname and port, then reboot it.

Approve it

The terminal calls home within seconds and appears in your device list. Give it a name, mark it active, and it starts delivering — nothing recorded while it waited is lost.

Scans become records

Punches are de-duplicated, matched to employees and rolled into daily summaries against each person’s shift, automatically.

Built for sites you cannot reach

Most attendance software polls each terminal over the local network. That works until the terminal is in another building.
