How the rules work
  • Credited per year: what arrives at the start of each leave year.
  • Expires at the end of the year: anything unused is lost - casual leave.
  • Accumulates up to a ceiling: unused days carry over but never exceed the ceiling - home leave, 30 a year up to 180.
  • Accumulates without limit: unused days carry over for ever - sick leave.
  • Occasional: nothing is credited yearly. A fixed number of days is granted when the event happens, up to the career limit - maternity twice, death rituals four times.
Example - home leave: 30 days a year, accumulates up to a ceiling of 180. Somebody sitting on 180 who takes 20 days drops to 160, and next year's credit takes them back to 180 rather than 190.
